A FAIR FIGHT

The ground rules.

Starting line
Equal starting balances in paper money
Same playground
AAPL · MSFT · NVDA · AMZN · GOOGL
Market data
Alpaca IEX · regular U.S. sessions
Same clock
One cycle every 10 seconds while open
Same strategy
Trend following on completed daily bars
Buying power
Cash only · long fractional shares · no leverage
At most 95% invested
Entry limits
2 new entries per session · 4-hour cooldown
Protective rules
8% daily loss stop · 2× ATR price stop
Retirement
Stop the bot at 60% loss from starting balance
Trading costs
5 bps modeled fee + 5 bps slippage per side
Spread filter
At most 25 basis points (0.25%)
API allowance
No daily spending cutoff · costs tracked

AI choices have more than one permitted action. Required holds ask only for signal strength. Protective exits are made by code. Option probabilities describe the choice, not the chance of making money.

Each bot sees the same stock indicators and its own portfolio. Daily trend signals use nine historical windows, plus one- and five-day momentum, volatility and ATR. The bots do not browse news or learn from earlier trades.

Positions may remain open overnight, but decisions and fills run only during regular sessions. Overnight gaps can exceed a stop price. This is one experiment, not a universal model ranking.
